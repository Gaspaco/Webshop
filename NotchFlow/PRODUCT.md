# Product

## Register

product

## Users

MacBook owners who listen to music while working and want useful playback and system status controls close to the notch without keeping another window open. The first release is for direct-download testers on modern macOS.

## Product Purpose

NotchFlow turns the otherwise unused notch area into a compact control surface. Version one provides glanceable now-playing information, playback controls, battery status, and output volume. Success means the interface stays out of the way when closed, opens instantly, and remains dependable across desktops and full-screen spaces.

## Brand Personality

Tactile, discreet, playful. The app should feel native to the Mac without impersonating an Apple interface or another notch utility.

## Anti-references

- An exact copy of Boring Notch's branding, layout, motion, or source code.
- Large dashboard panels that cover active work.
- Decorative glass effects, neon gradients, and controls without clear purpose.
- Interfaces that only work on one MacBook screen size.

## Design Principles

1. The closed island should disappear into the physical notch.
2. One gesture should reveal the controls that matter now.
3. Information must remain readable without making the panel feel like a dashboard.
4. Every animation communicates state and has a reduced-motion alternative.
5. Notch and notchless displays must both remain usable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, VoiceOver labels, sufficient text contrast, reduced motion, and status cues that do not depend on color alone. Controls must retain comfortable pointer targets despite the compact surface.
